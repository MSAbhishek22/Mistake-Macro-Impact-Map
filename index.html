The problem

One untraded death gives the other team map control. That forces a rotation, wastes your team's utility, and a few seconds later the round is gone — but the scoreboard only shows the last part.

The solution

The Mistake to Macro Impact Map traces a round loss back along the chain — the mistake, the map control it gave away, the rotation it forced — and shows the economic and control cost at each step.

The outcome

Instead of a bare 'my bad', you get the exact moment things went wrong and a tactical correction for next time, so the same mistake stops costing rounds.
